## What we do

We meet regularly to build and program robotic systems together. Our focus
is on everyday applications and playful projects. We work on our own
projects, share knowledge within the group and present our results
publicly.

The group is explicitly open to students without prior experience. We
teach practical basics in mechanics, electronics, programming and systems
design.

We are not a competition team and not a research lab.

## How it started

Applied Robotics TU Berlin was founded in November 2026 by three students
of TU Berlin.

It started with a private project: a robot arm that plays an online
opponent's moves on a real chessboard. Building it made one thing clear —
what makes getting into robotics hard is starting on your own, and it is
far more fun with several people around one table. That became the idea of
continuing it as an open group at TU Berlin.

## Organisation

We are a student association in formation. Registration at TU Berlin is in
progress. We are not an institution of Technische Universität Berlin.

- The group is based on statutes. Its highest body is the general meeting,
  which takes place once per semester.
- Membership is open to members of Berlin's universities and universities
  of applied sciences, as well as TU Berlin alumni.
- Membership is free of charge.
- The association is neutral with regard to party politics and religion.
